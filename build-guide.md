<h1>Mounting the rear wheel to new Axle</h1>
<ol>
  <li>Remove rear wheel <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sMZBpMj_JZo">How to Replace the Wheels on the Razor Carbon Lux Kick Scooter</a> 0:39-2:05</li>
  <li>Cut axle to 5.5 in</li>
  <li>Drill a hole 1 in in from the end of the axle</li>
  <li>Drill another hole 2 &frac38; in from the other end of the axle</li>
  <li>3D print the wheel in PLA with 100% infill then 3D print the tire in TPU.</li>
  <li>Add lots of Epoxy to the outer rim of the wheel then slide the tire over the wheel and let epoxy dry and harder for 24h</li>
  <li>Attach flange to wheel using the #6-32x5/8&rsquo;&rsquo; screws and the lock nuts.</li>
  <li>Slide the axle through the frame then the wheel then the frame again leaving the hole that is 1 in away from the axle sticking out on the right side.</li>
  <li>Align the wheel in the middle of the frame then attach the collars to either side of the frame so the axle does not slide</li>
  <li>Attach the set screw that goes through the flange then have it go through the hole on the axle</li>
</ol>

<h1>Mounting the gear to the axle</h1>
<ol>
  <li>3D print the gear mount in PLA 100% infill</li>
  <li>Then using the screws and nuts that came with the gear, mount the gear to the gearmount</li>
  <li>Mount the flange to the gear mount so that the screws are in the same direction as the screws for the gear.</li>
  <li>Slide the gear with everything on it with the flange side down</li>
  <li>Use the set screws to screw it in to the axle</li>
</ol>

<h1>Wiring the Electronics</h1>
<ol>
  <li>To the end of the motor solder the male end of the connector (the opposite of the one in the esc).</li>
  <li>Using the attachment piece on the battery, attach the female end of the connector (the one opposite the battery port on the esc).</li>
  <li>Plug the motor (blue and yellow wire) and battery (black and red wire) in to the esc then plug the key ignition in to the power locks end of the esc (pink and blue)</li>
  <li>Attach the thumb throttle to the plug it came with then solder the 3 port plug with the opposite end of the one on the throttle then plug that in</li>
</ol>

<h1>Mounting the Motor and adding the Chain</h1>
<ol>
  <li>Cut the chain to 97 links</li>
  <li>Rest the motor on the bed of the scooter and attach the chain to the motor and the gear</li>
  <li>Find a spot where the chain is straight and taught</li>
  <li>Mark the spot of the holes in the mounting bracket to the frame</li>
  <li>Drill the frame</li>
  <li>Add the screws to the motor but the screws are going upward then add the nuts and tighten</li>
  <li>Loop the chain around the motor and the gear</li>
  <li>Using velcro or double sided tape, attach the motor and esc to the scooter</li>
  <li>Take out the handle bar for the side you want the thumb throttle to be on.</li>
  <li>Cut the string then slide in the thumb throttle</li>
  <li>Reattach the handle bar and using tape wrap excess slack</li>
  <li>Using tape stick the ignition to the frame</li>
</ol>
